About Us

ArtifyPH is all about celebrating the creativity of local artists and crafters by giving them a platform to showcase their work to a much wider audience. The commission feature is designed to help artists get the exposure they need, get clients and go on to build really rewarding creative careers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this for?

ArtifyPH is for artists and crafters who want to showcase their work and get commissions This also includes crafters, designers, and people in many other creative fields. Users who want to browse or check out artists are also welcome!

How do I post and edit?

You can post your art on your profile. Click the “+” icon to add and “Edit” to edit.

How do I find other Artists?

You can find artists on the Homepage, which showcases different artists every week. Click the post, then the username, to go to their profile, where you can find their portfolio or commissions.

How do I commission Artists?

You can commission an artist by going to their profile. A button there will show “Request Commission.” This will take you to a form you must complete.

Why can't I commission some artists?

Some artists may not be accepting new commissions at the moment. You can check whether they are currently accepting commissions by visiting the Commission tab on their profile.

How do I pay?

You'll find payment options in the commission form after you complete it. We accept credit/debit cards and GCash as payment methods.

What happens after I pay?

The artist will be notified of the commission for review. If accepted, the artist will work on the commission with the deadline set. If the artist rejects it, you will receive a refund.

How do I know the status of my commissions?

You can check the status of your commissions in your account dashboard. You will also receive updates via email as the artist progresses on your commission.

How do I customize my profile and account information?

You can customize your profile photo and background by simply clicking on them. To edit your account information, go to your settings.
